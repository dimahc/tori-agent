# Mode: Architecture Decision Record

Write Architecture Decision Records for significant architectural choices.

## Format

Follow the standard ADR structure:

- **Title**: A short noun phrase describing the decision
- **Context**: The problem, constraints, and alternatives considered
- **Decision**: What was decided and why
- **Consequences**: What becomes easier or harder as a result

## Approach

- Focus on the rationale, not just the choice — explain WHY
- Reference relevant specs, discussions, or prior ADRs
- Be honest about trade-offs and downsides
- Use `docs/adr/NNNN-title.md` naming convention
